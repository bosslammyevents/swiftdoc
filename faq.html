How do I get started?

Go to our services, tap the one you need and click “I'd like to make enquiries”. WhatsApp opens with your message already typed. Just hit send and we'll guide you from there.

Do I need to visit your office?

For most services, no. We handle the process for you. A few services may need you to appear in person (e.g. biometrics for passports or NIN), and we'll tell you upfront. You're also welcome at our office on Opebi Road, Ikeja.

How long does it take?

It depends on the service and the government agency involved. We'll give you a timeline before you pay.

How much does it cost?

Prices vary by service. Send us an enquiry on WhatsApp and we'll give you a clear quote, with no hidden charges.

How do I pay?

Once you've confirmed the service, we'll send our official payment details on WhatsApp.

Yes. We keep you updated on WhatsApp until your document is ready.

What documents will I need to provide?

It depends on the service. Once you send an enquiry, we'll tell you exactly what to send before you start.
